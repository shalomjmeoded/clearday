Do! Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

Your tasks stay on your phone. We do not upload, read, sell, or share your task data.

Information We Store

Do! stores your tasks, lists, reminders, and settings locally on your device using an on-device database. Do! does not require an account and does not upload your task content to our servers.

Notifications

If you choose to use reminders, Do! asks for notification permission so your device can show local reminder notifications. Reminder content is scheduled on your device.

Backups, Export, and Import

Do! lets you export and import backup files. Backup files may contain your task, list, reminder, and settings data. You choose where to save or share those files, such as Files, AirDrop, email, or cloud storage.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Do! Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process purchase information, subscription status, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, app user identifiers, and device or app information needed to provide and restore Pro access.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your task content for advertising.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not read or upload your task content.

Third-Party Services

Do! uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Apple's App Store for in-app purchases. Their services may process purchase-related data under their own policies.

Deleting Your Data

Because Do! stores task data locally and does not create user accounts, deleting the app removes the local app data from your device. Export a backup first if you want to keep your tasks.

Children's Privacy

Do! is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact listed on Do!'s App Store page.

Changes

We may update this policy when Do! changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
